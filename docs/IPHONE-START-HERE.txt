BASE PATTERN DESK FOR IPHONE / IPAD — START HERE
================================================

Apple only lets an app onto an iPhone when it is signed with an Apple ID. This folder is the complete
Xcode project with the newest app inside; you sign and install it yourself in a few minutes.

WHAT YOU NEED
- Your Mac with Xcode (free, from the Mac App Store).
- Your Apple ID (a free one works).
- Your iPhone and a USB cable.

STEPS (first time, about 10 minutes)
1. Unzip this file anywhere, for example in Documents. Keep the folders together:
   the "ios" folder and the "node_modules" folder must stay side by side.
2. Open  ios/App/App.xcodeproj  (double-click). Xcode opens and downloads the Capacitor
   package by itself (a minute; you need internet).
3. Xcode → Settings → Accounts → "+" → Apple ID → sign in.
4. In the left column click "App" (the blue icon) → target "App" → tab "Signing & Capabilities":
   - tick "Automatically manage signing";
   - Team: choose your name (Personal Team);
   - if Xcode says the Bundle Identifier is taken, change it to something of your own,
     for example  com.yourname.basepatterndesk
5. Connect the iPhone with the cable, unlock it, tap "Trust this computer".
   On the iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode → on (the iPhone restarts).
6. At the top of Xcode choose your iPhone as the destination and press the ▶ (Run) button.
7. The first time, the iPhone asks to trust you as developer:
   Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → your Apple ID → Trust.
8. Open "Base Pattern Desk" on the iPhone. Allow notifications when asked.

GOOD TO KNOW
- With a free Apple ID the app stops opening after 7 days: connect the iPhone and press ▶ again
  (30 seconds). With the paid Apple Developer Program (99 USD/year) it lasts a year.
- Live prices, the screener, alerts and holdings notifications work in the app itself (no website
  needed). iOS pauses apps in the background, so checks run while the app is open.
- Your watchlists, holdings and settings live on each device separately. Copy them with
  Settings → Back up my data (on the Mac) and Restore a backup (on the iPhone), e.g. via AirDrop.
- Alternative without Xcode: the Home Screen app from your own free website (docs/PHONE.md, way A);
  it updates itself.
